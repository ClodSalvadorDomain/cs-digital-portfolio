I'm Clod Salvador, founder of CS Digital. I build the AI systems and automations that turn a business's busywork into its competitive edge, so leads get answered, nurtured, and closed without anyone having to babysit the process.

I've built full websites and funnels for brands like Roman Homes & Loans and Yatai Spa, a model-home visitor sign-in portal that routes leads straight into a CRM, and HTML email systems that keep prospects engaged long after they leave the page.

On the automation side, I've built AI chat agents that respond to Facebook leads in seconds, lead enrichment pipelines that score and route prospects automatically, drip email campaigns that nurture on autopilot, and behind-the-scenes syncs across Gmail, Asana, and content repurposing so nothing falls through the cracks.
